# 🛒 Swiggy Instamart Sales & Operations Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive **Business Intelligence and Data Analytics dashboard using Microsoft Power BI** to analyze Swiggy Instamart quick-commerce sales, order performance, revenue, delivery operations, product categories, customer trends, and operational KPIs.

This project demonstrates an end-to-end **Data Analytics workflow** including data cleaning, data transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, KPI development, interactive data visualization, performance analysis, and business insight generation.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project was to transform raw quick-commerce data into a **data-driven Business Intelligence solution** that helps stakeholders monitor:

- Sales and revenue performance
- Total orders and order trends
- Delivery time and operational efficiency
- On-time delivery performance
- Product category performance
- Customer purchasing trends
- City-wise performance
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Business growth opportunities

---

## 🔑 Key Business Analysis

- Analyzed **total orders, revenue, delivery time, and operational performance**.
- Evaluated **On-Time Delivery Performance** to understand delivery efficiency.
- Performed **sales and revenue analysis** across product categories.
- Identified **top-performing product categories** based on sales, orders, and quantity.
- Analyzed **customer trends, customer segments, and purchasing behavior**.
- Evaluated **order trends and revenue patterns** to identify business growth opportunities.
- Developed **KPI dashboards** for monitoring sales and operational metrics.
- Created interactive **slicers, filters, charts, tables, and KPI cards** for dynamic business analysis.
- Performed comparative analysis to identify **high-performing and low-performing categories**.
- Transformed operational data into **actionable business insights** for data-driven decision-making.
