# 🤖 AI Conversation & Product Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive **AI Conversation Analytics and Product Analytics dashboard** using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze user engagement, conversation behavior, product usage, customer satisfaction, and AI platform performance.

The dashboard transforms raw AI interaction data into **actionable business insights** through **data analysis, data visualization, KPI reporting, trend analysis, customer analytics, and interactive dashboard development**.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyze AI platform usage and identify key patterns in:

- User engagement and conversation activity
- Customer satisfaction and user experience
- AI product and model performance
- Platform and version usage
- Geographic user distribution
- Subscription plan adoption
- Conversation topics and user interests
- Response time and AI service performance
- Business and product growth opportunities

## 📊 Key KPIs

- Total Users
- Total Conversations
- Average Response Time
- Average Customer Satisfaction
- AI Platform Uptime
- User Engagement
- Conversation Volume
- Product Usage
- Subscription Plan Distribution

## 🔍 Key Business Analysis

- Analyzed **user engagement and conversation activity** to understand platform adoption.
- Evaluated **customer satisfaction metrics** to identify user experience trends.
- Performed **AI product and model usage analysis** across different versions.
- Analyzed **geographic user distribution** to identify high-usage regions.
- Evaluated **subscription plan performance** across Free, Plus, and Pro users.
- Identified **popular conversation topics** such as Education, Business, AI, and Health.
- Performed **trend analysis** on conversations, users, satisfaction, and platform performance.
- Compared **platform and product usage patterns** to identify business opportunities.
- Analyzed **response time and uptime metrics** to evaluate operational performance.
- Converted raw data into **actionable insights for data-driven decision-making**.

## 🌍 Key Insights

- India, USA, and Germany showed high user activity.
- AI model versions showed significant usage across the platform.
- Free, Plus, and Pro subscription plans showed varied user distribution.
- Education, Business, AI, and Health were among the major conversation categories.
- Geographic visualization helped identify regional patterns in user satisfaction and platform performance.
- KPI analysis provided a clear view of overall AI platform performance.

## 📈 Business Value

This dashboard demonstrates how **AI product data and customer interaction data** can be transformed into meaningful business intelligence. The analysis supports **product performance monitoring, customer experience improvement, user engagement analysis, operational monitoring, and data-driven business decisions**.
