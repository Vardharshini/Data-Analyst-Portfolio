# 📊 Zomato Sales & Delivery Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive **Business Intelligence and Data Analytics dashboard using Microsoft Power BI** to analyze Zomato food delivery sales, revenue, order trends, customer ratings, payment methods, city-wise performance, cuisine performance, and delivery operations.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end **Data Analytics workflow**, including data cleaning, data transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, KPI development, data visualization, and business insight generation.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project was to transform raw food delivery data into a **data-driven Business Intelligence solution** that helps stakeholders monitor:

- Sales and revenue performance
- Order volume and trends
- Customer ratings and satisfaction
- City-wise business performance
- Cuisine-wise sales performance
- Payment method distribution
- Delivery time and operational efficiency
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

---

## 🔑 Key Business Analysis

- Analyzed **total orders, total revenue, average order value, customer ratings, and delivery time**.
- Performed **sales and revenue analysis** across different cuisines and cities.
- Identified **high-performing cities and food categories** based on order volume and revenue.
- Analyzed **customer ratings and satisfaction metrics**.
- Evaluated **payment method distribution** across orders.
- Analyzed **order trends and revenue patterns** to identify business opportunities.
- Compared city-wise performance to identify **high-performing and low-performing markets**.
- Evaluated delivery time to understand **operational efficiency and service performance**.
- Converted raw data into **actionable business insights** for data-driven decision-making.
