# 👗 Zudio Retail Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive **Retail Sales Analytics, Business Intelligence, and Performance Monitoring dashboard using Microsoft Power BI** to analyze Zudio retail business performance.

The dashboard provides insights into **sales revenue, total quantity sold, profit margin, average order value (AOV), customer demographics, gender-wise purchasing behavior, product categories, city-wise sales performance, monthly sales trends, and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).**

This project demonstrates an end-to-end **Data Analytics workflow** including data cleaning, data transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, KPI development, interactive data visualization, business analysis, and data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project was to transform raw retail sales data into a **Business Intelligence solution** that helps stakeholders monitor:

- Sales and revenue performance
- Product quantity and sales volume
- Profit margin and profitability
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Gender-wise sales performance
- Category-wise performance
- City-wise sales performance
- Monthly sales trends
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

---

## 🔑 Key Business Analysis

- Analyzed **total sales, total quantity sold, average profit margin, and average order value**.
- Performed **sales and revenue analysis** across different product categories.
- Evaluated **customer purchasing behavior** across male and female customer segments.
- Identified **high-performing product categories** based on sales and quantity.
- Performed **city-wise sales analysis** to identify major sales hubs.
- Analyzed **monthly sales trends** to identify fluctuations and seasonal business opportunities.
- Compared category-wise performance to identify **high-performing and low-performing segments**.
- Evaluated profitability using **profit margin analysis**.
- Analyzed customer and product trends to generate **actionable business insights**.
- Developed KPI-driven reporting to support **data-driven decision-making**.
